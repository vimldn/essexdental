place implants, does not own clinics). YMYL health site, so E-E-A-T and accuracy are
the bar. Ten pillar hubs at `/guides/[slug]/`, one hundred spokes at `/blog/[slug]/`
(10 per hub), the implant treatment pages as commercial pillars at
`/services/[service]/`, and 12 (soon 16) town pages at `/location/[city]/`.

(About page already commits to this; keep it). Anonymised worked examples and
editorial-team byline only; credit a named clinician with GDC number only where real
clinical input exists.

## 4. Internal-linking rules (silo-tight, within-site only)

- Every spoke links UP once to its hub (`/guides/<hub>/`) with a varied descriptive
  anchor, and once to the most relevant commercial pillar in context: a service
  page, or a `/location/<town>/` page where the spoke has a local angle. Plus the
  matching CTA.
- Hubs link DOWN to their 10 spokes and to the relevant service pillar(s); they may
  link sideways to 1-2 adjacent hubs where noted (e.g. H1<->H6 on cost comparisons,
  H4<->H5, H7<->H8, H9<->H10).
- Location pages link to the services, to the nearest hubs (cost, suitability,
  choosing a dentist), and the matching CTA. No service x town combo pages.
- External links: max 2 per page, one per domain, cite a named authority (GDC,
  NHS, the manufacturer) on first mention only. YMYL claims must be attributable.

## 5. YMYL / E-E-A-T (audit gaps to close)

- Blog posts currently emit **no Article schema** (only FAQPage). Add
  `Article`/`MedicalWebPage` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage`, author `@id` to the
  editorial entity (`/about/#author`), `datePublished` + `dateModified`. Hubs get
  the same.
- Author byline = the editorial team entity (no invented person); credit a named
  GDC-registered clinician only where real clinical input exists.
- Keep the anonymised-worked-examples disclosure; never fabricate cases, stats, or
  reviews.
